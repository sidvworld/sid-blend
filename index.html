about me

hi, i'm siddharth. ever since i was young, i've been fascinated by landscape photography. during quarantine in 2021, when leaving the house was sparse, i sought to bring Earth's landscape in my own way back at home. i began taking on 3D modeling commissions in Blender for games and independent projects. i spent most of my time creating realistic environments and modeling in-game assets, gradually teaching myself new techniques through experience.

at some point it became normalized in the modeling community to buy a $50 Blender plugin to be efficient. when professional software and asset libraries became difficult to afford, i started building simple tools and plugins to streamline my own work. this led me to research photogrammetry, the process of translating 2D real-world images into 3d models. after months of iteration, i developed a gaussian splatting-based model that allowed me to generate realistic assets from just a handful of photographs. many of the rocks, plants, and natural details in my projects today come directly from the world around me.

so beyond creating art, i hope my work shows others that meaningful projects don't always require expensive tools or resources. with enough curiosity and tenacity, some of the most valuable skills come from working around limitations rather than avoiding them.
